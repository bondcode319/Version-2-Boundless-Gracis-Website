Learning is not decorative

BGCF is a young organisation. We are in Phase 1 of our Strategic Plan 2026–2030 — completing registration, installing systems and running a baseline survey — so the figures below are commitments and early counts rather than a decade of results. We would rather publish that plainly than dress up a number.

Findings from every review are tabled at the Board, and the following year’s work plan must show explicitly what changed as a result. A mid-term review is scheduled for 2028 and an independent end-of-plan evaluation for 2030.

Results framework — 2030 targets

Indicative targets from the Strategic Plan, to be finalised against the baseline survey. Annual work plans break each one down by year. All figures marked TBC.

1,000

Children in school

Supported to enrol and remain enrolled, with 90% completing the level they entered.

5,000

Children reached with health outreach

Screened, treated or referred through camps run with district health teams.

6,000

People with safe water access

Through new or rehabilitated water points, with hygiene promotion alongside.

300

Youth through vocational training

Completing training and in work or running an enterprise afterwards.

400

Protection cases closed positively

Opened, managed and closed with a documented case plan and outcome.

800

Caregiver households saving

In functioning savings groups, with 70% reporting increased income at 12 months.

20

Child protection committees

Community committees established, trained and demonstrably active.

5

Unqualified audit opinions

One per year, every year of the plan. This is the target we are least willing to miss.
