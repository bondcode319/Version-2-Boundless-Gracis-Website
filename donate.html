How to give, today

Our online donation platform is being set up. Until it is live, every one of these routes reaches the same account and the same acknowledgement.

  1. Home
  2. /
  3. Donate

Three ways to give now

Tell us which programme you want your gift to fund — health, education or livelihoods — and it is coded to that fund in our accounts and reported against it.

Email us

Write to us with the amount and the fund you have in mind. We reply with the bank and mobile-money details, and a receipt once the gift lands.

Start by email

Call the office

Monday to Friday, 8:30am–5:00pm East Africa Time. Useful if you are giving from within Uganda or want to arrange a standing order.

+256 773 130 832

Use the enquiry form

Prefer to write it all down? The contact form reaches the same inbox, and you can ask for the due-diligence pack at the same time.

Open the form

A note on transparency. No card data is handled anywhere on this site. Gifts are recorded against our results framework, reviewed quarterly by the Board, and reported in the annual report and audited accounts — see Our Impact.

What your gift is coded to

  • Health & WASHThe consultation, the medicine, the referral transport and the follow-up visit — plus safe water points and hygiene promotion. We Heal.
  • EducationFees, uniform, scholastic materials and remedial support, with a named case worker following each child. We Educate.
  • Livelihoods & protectionSavings groups, enterprise training and start-up grants, so a household can carry its own children. We Empower.
  • The Mpara clinicBricks, cement, sand and the solar cold chain for a facility that removes the half-day walk entirely. Build a Clinic.

Regular beats large

A monthly gift is the only income a case worker can plan a school year or a treatment course around. Sponsorship is the simplest way to set one up.
