Trip overview

Useful hands, honestly deployed

Twice a year BGCF hosts a small team — never more than twelve — for a ten-day outreach based at Mpara. Days are spent supporting screening camps, classroom sessions, savings-group training and household visits, always paired with the Ugandan staff member who owns that work.

We are direct about the model. Volunteers do not run programmes, do not lead on clinical decisions outside their registration, and do not have unsupervised contact with children. If your skill does not fit a real gap in that fortnight, we will tell you so and offer a different way to help.

What to expect

  • Long days, simple conditions. Shared guesthouse rooms, bucket showers on some days, generator power in the evening, no reliable mobile data at site.
  • Safeguarding is not a formality. Enhanced background check, signed policy, no photographs of children, no unsupervised contact, and a named safeguarding lead on the team at all times.
  • Registration matters. Clinicians practise only within the scope the Uganda Medical and Dental Practitioners Council permits, under a Ugandan supervising clinician.
  • One free day. A Sunday for rest, worship if you wish, and a short trip to Kibale — arranged, not improvised.
  • A debrief you contribute to. The final evening is a structured review; what you observed goes into next year’s work plan.

Your investment

$1,450 per person · TBC

The package covers

  • Airport transfers and all in-country ground transport
  • Ten nights’ accommodation and three meals a day
  • Bottled or filtered drinking water throughout
  • In-country coordination, interpreters and safeguarding supervision
  • Sunday excursion and the pre-departure briefing pack

Not included

  • International flights
  • Uganda visa and any transit visas
  • Vaccinations and antimalarial prophylaxis
  • Travel and medical insurance (mandatory, proof required)
  • Background check fees and personal spending

How it works

  1. 01

    Apply

    Complete the form below. We read every application ourselves and reply within ten working days — including when the answer is no.

  2. 02

    Approval & deposit

    A short video call, two references and a background check. On clearance, a $400 deposit confirms your place; the balance is due six weeks before departure.

  3. 03

    Flights & vaccinations

    Book into Entebbe on the agreed arrival day. Yellow fever certificate is required for entry; your travel clinic will advise on the rest.
