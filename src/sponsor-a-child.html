The commitment

From $38 a month, you can send a child to school

Sponsorship covers school fees, uniform, shoes, scholastic materials, examination costs and remedial learning support for one child for a full academic year. It also funds the case worker who visits the household, tracks attendance and results, and intervenes before a child drops out rather than after.

We ask for a monthly commitment rather than a one-off gift because continuity is what carries a child through the transition to secondary school — the point at which most children in our communities leave education, and where girls leave first. Priority goes to girls and to children with disabilities.

$38

Per month · primary

Fees, uniform, materials and remedial support for one primary-school child.

$62

Per month · secondary

Boarding-level fees and materials, plus career guidance and life-skills sessions.

$85

Per month · vocational

Tuition, tools and a start-up kit for an out-of-school young person learning a trade.
