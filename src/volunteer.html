Who we are

A small organisation with a long horizon

BGCF is a Ugandan non-profit registered as a company limited by guarantee, working with orphaned and vulnerable children and their caregivers in Kyegegwa District. Our programmes are run by Ugandan staff. Volunteers extend that team’s reach; they do not substitute for it.

The most valuable contributions we receive are unglamorous: a bookkeeper who sets up our chart of accounts, a monitoring specialist who designs the baseline survey, a teacher who trains our remedial-learning volunteers, a translator who makes a safeguarding leaflet readable in Runyankore.

Mission archive

Every mission is written up, numbers and all — including the years that fell short of what we planned.

Our largest outreach to date ran across two parishes over eleven days. The team screened for malnutrition every morning and ran a maternal health clinic in the afternoons; the second week shifted to household visits with the case-work team, which is where most of the referrals came from. The honest lesson: we under-resourced the pharmacy on days four and five and had to turn patients away for a return visit. Stock planning changed as a result.
