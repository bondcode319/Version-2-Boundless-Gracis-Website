Who we are

From survival to possibility

Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation Limited is a non-profit, non-partisan, faith-inspired child-focused organisation incorporated in the Republic of Uganda as a company limited by guarantee, not having a share capital. Our registered office is at Mpara Village, Kyegegwa District.

The Foundation exists to walk with orphaned, abandoned and otherwise vulnerable children — and the households that care for them — from a place of survival to a place of possibility.

Our participants are orphaned and vulnerable children, children with disabilities, children at risk of or withdrawn from child labour and early marriage, and the caregivers who hold their families together: most often single mothers, grandmothers and elderly guardians.

Our vision and mission

Our vision

A world where every child grows up safe, healthy, educated and free to become everything they were created to be.

Our mission

To empower vulnerable children through quality education, holistic care and sustainable opportunities that nurture hope, unlock potential and create lasting transformation for individuals, families and communities.

Our three pillars

Vulnerability in a child’s life is rarely single-stranded. These three pillars are delivered as one package, held together by child protection and family strengthening running through all of them.

We Heal

  • Community health outreaches and medical camps
  • Nutrition screening and supplementary feeding
  • Immunisation and maternal health referral
  • Safe water points, sanitation and hygiene

We Educate

  • Child sponsorship, fees and scholastic support
  • Early childhood development and remedial learning
  • Vocational and technical skills training
  • Mentorship, life skills and career guidance

We Empower

  • Village savings and loan associations
  • Enterprise training and start-up grants
  • Climate-smart agriculture and farmer field schools
  • A women’s skilling centre for caregivers

Our values

These are drawn from our Constitution. They are written as tests we can fail, not as adjectives.

  • DignityNo child is described as helpless, and no child is excluded on grounds of sex, disability, tribe, faith or family background. Children are capable people in difficult circumstances.
  • IntentionalityEvery activity traces to a strategic objective and an indicator. If we cannot say what a piece of work is meant to change, we do not start it.
  • IntegrityDual signatories, segregation of duties, approved procurement thresholds, internal and external audit, and a whistleblower mechanism protected from retaliation.
  • TransparencyAudited accounts and annual programme reports are presented to the Board, filed with the NGO Bureau and published here — including the years we miss a target.
  • AccountabilityTo children first, then to communities, government and donors. Our Designated Safeguarding Lead reports directly to the Board’s Safeguarding Committee so no concern can be suppressed at management level.

Our Constitution also commits us to Child First, Compassion, Inclusion, Partnership and Stewardship — the full statement of values sits in Article 3 and is available on request.
