Field note · Education

What a term’s scholastic materials actually cost

Exercise books, pens, a uniform and a pair of shoes. The list is short, the total is not, and it is the reason a child stops coming to class.

Field note · Health

Why we count the follow-up, not the camp

Treating a child once is an activity. Seeing the same child three weeks later and finding them recovered is the only version of the number that means anything — and it is harder, slower and less photogenic to collect.
