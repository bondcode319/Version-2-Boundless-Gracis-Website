Structure

Three layers, one accountability line

The Board of Trustees is the supreme organ and custodian of the Constitution: it clears directors, evaluates board performance, arbitrates disputes and may veto any decision that departs from our mission. The Board of Directors governs — it appoints and supervises the Executive Director, approves the strategy, work plan, budget, policies and audited accounts, and carries ultimate responsibility for safeguarding.

Five directorates form the Senior Management Team. Child Protection and Safeguarding reports operationally to the Executive Director but has a direct, unobstructed line to the Board’s Safeguarding Committee, so that no concern can be suppressed at management level.

Appointments in progress. Boundless Gracia is completing incorporation and NGO Bureau registration. Names are published only once an appointment is formally minuted, so the cards below carry roles and remits, with names to be confirmed. The Foundation was founded by Asiimwe Eunice in 2024.

Joining the team

Every role is filled through a safeguarding-compliant process

Vetting first

References, identity and relevant checks are completed, and the safeguarding and child protection policies are signed, before a start date is agreed.

Representation

At least one third of the Board of Directors are women, and we recruit locally wherever a role can be held by someone from the communities we serve.

Volunteers too

Volunteers go through the same checks as staff. Skills are matched to a real gap in the work plan, not to a visit someone would like to make.

We are recruiting, and we are recruiting for safeguarding

If you want to work or volunteer with Boundless Gracia, tell us what you do and we will tell you honestly where it fits.
