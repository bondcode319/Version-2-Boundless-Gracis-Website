Our vision and mission

Our vision

“A world where every child grows up safe, healthy, educated, and free to reach their full potential.”

Our mission

“To empower vulnerable children through quality education, holistic care and sustainable opportunities that nurture hope, unlock potential, and create lasting transformation for individuals, families, and communities.”

Who we are

From survival to possibility

Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation Limited is a non-profit, non-partisan, child-focused organisation incorporated in the Republic of Uganda as a company limited by guarantee, not having a share capital. Our registered office is at Mpara Village, Kyegegwa District.

We work at the point where poverty, family breakdown and limited services meet in a child’s life, and we respond with an integrated package of education, health, protection and household economic strengthening rather than relying on a single isolated intervention.

Our primary participants are orphaned and vulnerable children, children with disabilities, children at risk of or withdrawn from child labour and early marriage, teenage mothers, and the caregivers who hold their families together — most often single mothers, grandmothers and elderly guardians.

Core values

Six values, written as tests we can fail

Values are only useful if they can be checked. Each of ours names a thing we must actually do, not an adjective we would like applied to us.

  • Child First

    Every decision the Foundation takes is tested first against the best interests of the child.
  • Integrity and Accountability

    We account openly for every shilling and every promise, to donors, communities and children alike.
  • Compassion

    We serve with warmth, patience and respect for the dignity of every person we meet.
  • Dignity and Inclusion

    No child is excluded on grounds of sex, disability, tribe, faith or family background.
  • Partnership

    Lasting change is built with communities and government, not delivered to them.
  • Stewardship

    Resources entrusted to us are managed prudently, transparently and for maximum impact.

Our objectives

Five objectives that turn the mission into work

  1. Skills for life and work

    To empower underprivileged children by equipping them with essential life skills and vocational training.

  2. Education regardless of circumstance

    To ensure every child, irrespective of their social economic background, has access to quality education, scholastic materials, and school retention support — for orphaned, abandoned and otherwise vulnerable children and youth.

  3. Equal opportunity

    To build capacity in the eradication of gender-based discrimination and the provision of equal opportunities.

  4. Health and wellbeing

    To assist in access to and provision of vital emotional and psychological support, nutrition support, immunisation outreach, and the provision of safe water, sanitation and hygiene services.

  5. Protection from harm

    To protect children from abuse, neglect, exploitation, child labour, trafficking and early marriage — through prevention, case management, referral and family-based alternative care.

Programme areas

What each department actually does

Four departments, one child. Protection and family strengthening run through all of the others rather than sitting beside them.

Boundless Gracia programme areas and the activities each department delivers
Department What we do
Education and Child Development Child sponsorship, school fees and scholastic support, early childhood development, remedial learning, vocational and technical skills training, mentorship and life skills.
Health, Nutrition and WASH Community health outreaches, nutrition screening, supplementary feeding, immunisation and maternal health referral, safe water points, sanitation and hygiene promotion, and menstrual health education and supplies.
Child Protection and Family Strengthening Case identification and management, psychosocial support, family tracing and reintegration, kinship and foster care support, community child protection committees, and safeguarding awareness.
Community Livelihoods and Empowerment Village savings and loan associations, enterprise and financial literacy training, start-up and top-up grants, climate-smart agriculture and farmer field schools, and a women’s skilling centre.
