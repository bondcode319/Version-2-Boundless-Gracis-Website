Who we are

A foundation built around the child, not the programme

At Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation we believe every child deserves to be seen, valued, loved and given the opportunity to build a better future. The Foundation was established in 2024 by Asiimwe Eunice, with a heartfelt commitment to support vulnerable children, teenage mothers and children with special needs — particularly those growing up in disadvantaged communities.

We work at the point where poverty, family breakdown and limited services meet in a child’s life, and we respond with an integrated package of education, health, protection and household economic strengthening rather than one isolated intervention.

Vision and mission

From survival to possibility

Our vision is a world where every child grows up safe, healthy, educated and free to reach their full potential.

Our mission is to empower vulnerable children through quality education, holistic care and sustainable opportunities that nurture hope, unlock potential and create lasting transformation for individuals, families and communities.

Our participants are orphaned and vulnerable children, children with disabilities, teenage mothers, children at risk of or withdrawn from child labour and early marriage — and the caregivers who hold their families together, most often single mothers, grandmothers and elderly guardians.

Our values & objectives

What we do

Four programme areas, delivered as one package

Vulnerability in a child’s life is rarely single-stranded. A child out of school is usually also a child who is hungry, unwell, unprotected, or living in a household with no reliable income — so we treat all four together.

We Educate

Child sponsorship, school fees and scholastic support, early childhood development, remedial learning, vocational and technical training, mentorship and life skills.

Education & child development

We Heal

Community health outreaches, nutrition screening and supplementary feeding, immunisation and maternal referral, safe water points, sanitation and hygiene.

Health, nutrition & WASH

We Protect

Protection from abuse, neglect, exploitation, child labour, trafficking and early marriage — through prevention, case management, referral and family-based alternative care.

Child protection & safeguarding

We Empower

Village savings and loan associations, enterprise and financial literacy training, start-up grants, climate-smart agriculture and a women’s skilling centre for caregivers.
