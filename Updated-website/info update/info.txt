Mission :
To empower vulnerable children thru quality education, holistic care and sustainable opportunities that nurture hope, unlock potential and create lasting transformation for individuals, families and communities About Us

Extending Grace. Restoring Hope. Transforming Lives.

At Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation, we believe that every child deserves to be seen, valued, loved, and given the opportunity to build a better future.

Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation was founded by Asiimwe Eunice in 2024. It was established with a heartfelt commitment to support vulnerable children, teenage mothers, and children with special needs, particularly those growing up in disadvantaged communities.

We exist because a child’s circumstances should never determine the limits of their future.

Our Story

Boundless Gracia was born from a desire to extend compassion and practical support to children and families who face difficult circumstances.

We believe that grace is more than a word—it is compassion put into action.

Through education, wellbeing support, basic needs assistance, skills development, and community initiatives, we seek to create opportunities for children to learn, grow, regain hope, and discover their potential.

Our work is not simply about providing temporary assistance. It is about walking alongside children and families, restoring dignity, nurturing potential, and helping create pathways toward a brighter future.
