How it began

A desire to extend compassion, and the practical support to go with it

At Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation, we believe that every child deserves to be seen, valued, loved, and given the opportunity to build a better future.

The Foundation was founded by Asiimwe Eunice in 2024. It was established with a heartfelt commitment to support vulnerable children, teenage mothers, and children with special needs — particularly those growing up in disadvantaged communities.

We exist because a child’s circumstances should never determine the limits of their future.

Our story

Walking alongside, not passing through

Boundless Gracia was born from a desire to extend compassion and practical support to children and families who face difficult circumstances.

Through education, wellbeing support, basic needs assistance, skills development and community initiatives, we seek to create opportunities for children to learn, grow, regain hope and discover their potential.

Our work is not simply about providing temporary assistance. It is about walking alongside children and families, restoring dignity, nurturing potential, and helping create pathways toward a brighter future.

Who we serve

The children and caregivers at the centre of the work

We work at the point where poverty, family breakdown and limited services meet in a child’s life — and we respond with an integrated package rather than a single isolated intervention.

Orphaned and vulnerable children

Children who have lost one or both parents, or who are living in households that cannot reliably meet their basic needs.

Children with special needs

Children with disabilities, who are excluded from school first and returned to it last, and whose caregivers carry the heaviest costs.

Teenage mothers

Young mothers whose own education stopped abruptly, supported back into learning, skills training or an enterprise of their own.

The caregivers who hold it together

Most often single mothers, grandmothers and elderly guardians — strengthened so a household can carry its own children.

How we work

From a first household visit to a child who no longer needs us

  1. Identify

    Cases reach us through community leaders, schools, local government and household visits. Each one is assessed against documented vulnerability criteria, not impression.

  2. Plan with the family

    A case worker agrees a plan with the caregiver covering school, health, protection and income — because the four strands move together or not at all.

  3. Deliver and follow up

    Support is delivered, recorded and followed up. Attendance, results, nutrition status and household income are all tracked over time, not counted once.

  4. Step back

    The aim is a household that can carry its children without us. Exit is planned, agreed and recorded — it is a success, not a withdrawal.

What we promise

Three things we will not trade away

  • The child’s dignity. No child is described as helpless and no child is used as a fundraising prop. We publish no identifiable image or personal detail without documented, informed guardian consent.
  • Honest accounting. We account openly for every shilling and every promise — to donors, to communities and to children alike.
  • Family-based care. We strengthen kinship and foster care and support reunification. Boundless Gracia does not run, and will not build, a residential institution for children.
