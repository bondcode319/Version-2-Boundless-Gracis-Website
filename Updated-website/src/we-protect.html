The problem

Harm to a child is rarely a single event

Child labour, trafficking and early marriage do not usually begin with a stranger. They begin with a household under pressure, a school fee that cannot be found, a guardian who falls ill, and a community that has no safe route to raise a concern.

That is why protection work at Boundless Gracia is not a hotline bolted onto a programme. It is case identification, a named case worker, a documented plan, a referral path into the statutory system, and a community structure that keeps working after we leave the village.

What we do

Five activities, one continuous system

  • Case identification and management, with a documented case plan and a recorded outcome for every child.
  • Psychosocial and emotional support, including for teenage mothers and children withdrawn from labour or early marriage.
  • Family tracing, reunification, and kinship and foster care support — family-based care, never institutional care.
  • Community child protection committees and para-social workers established, trained and kept active.
  • Safeguarding awareness in schools and communities, with a reporting route everyone knows how to use.

How we hold ourselves to it

Safeguarding is a governance matter, not a programme preference. These rules are not ours to waive in the field.

The 24-hour rule

Any safeguarding concern is reported within 24 hours and referred to the Police and the District Probation and Social Welfare Officer where the law requires it. There is no internal discretion to delay.

A line that cannot be blocked

Our Designated Safeguarding Lead reports operationally to the Executive Director but has a direct, unobstructed line to the Board’s Safeguarding Committee, so no concern can be suppressed at management level.

Vetting before a start date

Every staff member, volunteer and visiting partner is vetted and signs the safeguarding and child protection policies before any contact with children is agreed.

Raising a concern

If a child may be at risk, tell us today

How to report. Write SAFEGUARDING in the first line of an email to boundlessgracialtd@gmail.com, or call +256 773 130 832, and the concern is escalated the same day. Reports may be made anonymously, and our whistleblower protection means no one who reports in good faith may be retaliated against. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the Police first.
