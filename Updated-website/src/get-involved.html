Where the money goes

Every gift is allocated to the fund you choose, spent against an approved work plan, and reported against an indicator in the annual report.

How children are protected

Vetting before contact, the 24-hour reporting rule, consent before publicity, and a safeguarding line that bypasses management.

What we got wrong

Our reporting names missed targets as well as met ones. Ask for the due-diligence pack and judge for yourself.
