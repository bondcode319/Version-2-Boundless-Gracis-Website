What a field week is

A work plan with people attached, not a trip.

A field week is a scheduled block of field work: an outreach day run with district health teams, a scholastic materials distribution, household assessment visits, or a training block at the skilling centre. The schedule is set by the programme, not by the visitors.

You will be given a role that matches what you can actually do, a team to do it with, and a briefing that covers safeguarding, consent, photography and the limits of your role. If what you offer does not match a planned activity, we will say so rather than invent one.

Field weeks are small by design. A large group costs the programme more in logistics than it contributes in work.

A typical day

What the days actually look like

  1. Early start

    Load the vehicle, brief the team, confirm the site with the local council and the district health team, and set up before families arrive on foot.

  2. Registration and triage

    Children and caregivers are registered against the programme record. Screening and triage decide the order of the day, not arrival time.

  3. The work itself

    Consultations, screening and feeding, distributions, or training. Volunteers work to their brief and under the supervision of the responsible staff member.

  4. Close and record

    Pack down, record every child seen against the results framework, and agree the follow-up list. The paperwork is the part that makes the day count.

Practicalities

What it costs and what to expect

You cover your own costs

Flights, visa, insurance, vaccinations, accommodation and in-country transport are yours. Boundless Gracia charges no field-week or volunteer fee, and no part of your trip cost reaches the programme — so please do not count it as a donation.

Vetting before travel

References, identity checks and signature of the safeguarding and child protection policies are completed before a place is confirmed. There are no exceptions, including for short visits.

Conditions in the field

Rural Kyegegwa District: unsealed roads, limited power and water at some sites, long days and hot weather. Accommodation is simple and shared.

Dates. Field-week dates are set against the programme calendar, usually one or two blocks a year. Register your interest and we will tell you the next confirmed window and what skills it needs.

Remote volunteering — grants, bookkeeping, monitoring and evaluation, translation — often contributes more than a week in the field, and costs the programme nothing.
