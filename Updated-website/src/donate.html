Where it goes

What each fund is coded to

  • Education

    Fees, uniform, scholastic materials and remedial support, with a named case worker following each child. We Educate.
  • Health, nutrition and WASH

    The consultation, the medicine, the referral transport and the follow-up visit — plus safe water points and hygiene promotion. We Heal.
  • Child protection

    Case management, psychosocial support, family tracing and the community protection committees that keep working after we leave. We Protect.
  • Livelihoods

    Savings groups, enterprise training and start-up grants, so a household can carry its own children. We Empower.
  • The Mpara clinic appeal

    A restricted fund: bricks, cement, the maternal unit and the solar cold chain for a facility that removes the half-day walk entirely. Build a Clinic.
  • Wherever the need is greatest

    Unrestricted. It pays for the things restricted funding rarely covers — the finance officer, the audit, the vehicle that reaches the outreach site.
