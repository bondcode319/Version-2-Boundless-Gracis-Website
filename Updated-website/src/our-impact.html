Where we are

A young organisation, counting honestly

Boundless Gracia was founded in 2024 and is in Phase 1 of its Strategic Plan 2026–2030: completing registration, installing systems and running a baseline survey. The figures below are therefore commitments and early counts rather than a decade of results. We would rather publish that plainly than dress up a number.

Findings from every review are tabled at the Board, and the following year’s work plan must show explicitly what changed as a result. A mid-term review is scheduled for 2028 and an independent end-of-plan evaluation for 2030.

How we count

Three rules behind every number on this page

One child, counted once

A child who receives education, health and protection support appears once in the headline figure, with the detail in the breakdown. Stacking services to inflate a reach number is a reporting failure, not a success.

Outcome, not activity

“Trained 200 caregivers” is an activity. “140 households reported higher income twelve months later” is an outcome. We publish the second, which takes a year longer to say.

Published even when it disappoints

Where we miss a target, the annual report says so, says why, and says what the following year’s work plan changes as a result.
