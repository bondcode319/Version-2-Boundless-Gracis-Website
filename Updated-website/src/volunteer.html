How we work with volunteers

A real job, or no job

Every volunteer role at Boundless Gracia exists because there is a gap in an approved work plan. We do not create activities for visitors, and we do not place volunteers in roles that a Ugandan professional should hold — recruiting locally is part of the point.

Volunteers go through the same checks as staff: references, identity and relevant vetting, and signature of the safeguarding and child protection policies before a start date is agreed. Nobody has unsupervised one-to-one contact with a child.

Remote volunteering is often the most useful of all. Grant writing, bookkeeping, monitoring and evaluation design, translation and website work can be done from anywhere and cost us nothing in logistics.

Skills we need

What is genuinely useful right now

This list changes. If your skill is not here, write anyway — we will tell you whether and when it would help.

Clinical

Clinical officers, nurses, midwives and nutritionists for outreach days, working alongside district health teams under their protocols.

Teaching and learning

Teachers for remedial sessions, early years specialists, and trainers for vocational and life-skills courses.

Social work

Social workers and counsellors with child protection experience, to strengthen case management and psychosocial support.

Finance and systems

Accountants, auditors and people who can build a clean chart of accounts, a procurement file or a payroll process.

Grants and M&E

Grant writers, and monitoring and evaluation specialists who can turn a results framework into something a field team can actually fill in.

Trades and agriculture

Builders, water technicians, tailors, caterers and agricultural extension workers for the skilling centre and farmer field schools.

Ground rules

What every volunteer agrees to

  • Complete vetting and sign the safeguarding and child protection policies before any contact with children.
  • No photography of children, and no posting to social media, without documented guardian consent routed through the office.
  • No unsupervised one-to-one contact with a child, and no private contact details exchanged with a child or family.
  • No gifts, cash or promises made directly to an individual child or household — everything goes through the case worker.
  • Report any safeguarding concern within 24 hours, including concerns about another volunteer or a member of staff.

Tell us what you do

We answer every message within five working days, including the ones where the honest answer is “not this quarter”. Registering is not an application and places you under no obligation.
