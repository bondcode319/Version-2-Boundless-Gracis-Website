Legal status

What Boundless Gracia is, legally

Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation Limited is a non-profit, non-partisan, child-focused organisation founded in 2024 and incorporated in the Republic of Uganda as a company limited by guarantee, not having a share capital, under the Companies Act No. 1 of 2012. Our registered office is at Mpara Village, Kyegegwa District.

The Foundation has no shareholders and distributes no profits. Any surplus is applied to the objects set out in our Constitution. Incorporation and NGO Bureau registration are being completed; certificate numbers are published here as they are issued.

Due diligence welcome. Our constitution, organogram, strategic plan, safeguarding and child protection policies, finance and procurement manual and audited accounts are available on request, and we will complete your due-diligence questionnaire. Write to boundlessgracialtd@gmail.com.

Governance structure

Who decides what

Three layers, each with a defined remit, and a safeguarding line that bypasses management entirely.

Governance bodies, their composition and their responsibilities
Body Composition and remit
Board of Trustees Up to five members; meets at least twice a year. The supreme organ and custodian of the Constitution. Vets and clears directors, evaluates board performance, arbitrates disputes, and may veto any decision that departs from the mission and vision.
Board of Directors Five directors, at least one third of them women, cleared by the Trustees. Appoints and supervises the Executive Director; approves the strategic plan, annual work plan, budget, policies and audited accounts; monitors delivery quarterly; bears ultimate responsibility for safeguarding.
Board committees Finance, Audit and Administration; Programmes; and Safeguarding. The Safeguarding Committee receives reports directly from the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
Senior Management Team Five directorates — Finance and Administration; Education and Child Development; Health, Nutrition and WASH; Child Protection and Safeguarding; Community Livelihoods and Empowerment — led by the Executive Director, who is accounting officer and ex-officio Board Secretary.

Financial control

What stops money going missing

  • Dual signatories on every bank account, with the Executive Director as principal signatory and no single-person payment authority.
  • Segregation of duties between requisition, approval, payment and recording.
  • Approved procurement thresholds, with competitive quotation above the lowest tier and a documented evaluation.
  • Restricted funds coded to the fund the donor chose, and reported against that code.
  • An annual external audit, presented to the Board, filed with the regulator and published here.

Safeguarding

The rules that are not ours to waive

Safeguarding at Boundless Gracia is a Board responsibility. Field convenience never overrides it.

Report within 24 hours

Any safeguarding concern is reported within 24 hours and referred to the Police and the District Probation and Social Welfare Officer where the law requires. There is no internal discretion to delay.

A line to the Board

The Designated Safeguarding Lead reports operationally to the Executive Director but has a direct, unobstructed line to the Board’s Safeguarding Committee.

Everyone is vetted

Staff, volunteers and visiting partners are vetted and sign the policy before any contact with children is agreed.

Consent before publicity

No identifiable image or personal detail of a child is published anywhere, including this website, without documented informed guardian consent, which may be withdrawn.

Whistleblower protection

Reports may be made anonymously. Anyone reporting a concern in good faith is protected from retaliation, and retaliation is itself a disciplinary matter.

No residential care

We strengthen kinship and foster care and support reunification. Boundless Gracia does not run, and will not build, a residential institution for children.
