The case

Outreaches shorten the distance. A facility removes it.

A mobile outreach reaches a village for a day, treats what it can see, refers what it cannot, and leaves. It is the right answer to a lack of coverage, and the wrong answer to a lack of permanence.

A permanent outpatient and maternal health unit at Mpara changes the arithmetic for every household in the catchment: antenatal care that can be attended more than once, a delivery that does not have to happen at home after dark, and treatment for a fever on the day it starts rather than the week it becomes an emergency.

The facility is being planned in coordination with the district health authorities, so that it joins the public system rather than competing with it, and is staffed and supplied on terms the district can sustain.

The plan

Four phases, each one usable on its own

The appeal is deliberately structured so that money raised is spent on a phase that works, rather than held until a total is reached. Costed budgets for each phase are available on request.

Phase 1 — Site and approvals

Land secured and titled, district health approvals, architectural drawings and the environmental and safeguarding assessments.

Phase 2 — Shell and outpatient

Foundation, walls, roof and the outpatient consultation rooms, so that the building begins seeing patients before it is finished.

Phase 3 — Maternal unit

Delivery room, waiting area for expectant mothers travelling in, and the water, sanitation and waste systems a maternity service requires.

Phase 4 — Power, cold chain, equipment

Solar power, the vaccine cold chain, basic diagnostic equipment and the first stock of essential medicines.

What we promise about this appeal

  • Restricted funds stay restricted. Gifts to the clinic appeal are coded to the clinic fund and cannot be moved to another budget line without the Board recording the decision and telling the donors who gave to it.
  • No launch photograph before a plan. We publish the costed budget and the approvals before we publish an artist’s impression.
  • Procurement is competitive. Construction and equipment above the approved threshold go to competitive quotation with a documented evaluation.
  • If it cannot proceed, we say so. Should the project prove undeliverable, every donor to the fund is contacted and given the choice of redirection or return.
