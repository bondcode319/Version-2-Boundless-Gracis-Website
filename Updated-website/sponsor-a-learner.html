Why monthly

Steady support keeps a child steady in school

Fees, uniform and materials fall due on the school calendar. A monthly gift meets each of them on time. And because it arrives every month, a case worker can respond the week malaria keeps a child at home or a guardian’s income suddenly stops, without first having to find the money.

That is why we ask for a monthly commitment rather than a one-off gift. Continuity is what keeps a child enrolled through the transition to secondary school, which is exactly where most drop out — and where girls drop out first.

You will receive termly reporting on attendance, results and wellbeing, written by the child’s case worker. We do not publish an identifiable image or personal detail of any child without documented guardian consent, and that applies to the reports we send you as much as to this website.

What it covers

What your monthly gift covers

A primary place is budgeted at UGX [TBC: costed monthly primary sponsorship] (about US$[TBC]) a month. Sponsorship is pooled at the school and term level so that no child is left short because one sponsor’s payment is late. The breakdown below is what a sponsored place is budgeted to buy.

Fees and exams

School fees for the term, examination fees and the school’s own charges, paid directly to the school, never in cash to a household.

Scholastic materials

Exercise books, pens, mathematical set, and the textbooks a class is expected to supply for itself.

Uniform and shoes

A uniform that fits, replaced as a child grows, plus shoes — which is more often than not the item that decides whether a child is sent home.

Case worker support

Household visits, remedial learning where results slip, and the follow-up that happens before a child disappears from the register.

Secondary and vocational places cost more than primary ones. We quote the exact figure when we match you, rather than publishing an average that is wrong for every individual child.

How it works

From first enquiry to first report

  1. Tell us

    Complete the form below. Say whether you would like to sponsor a primary, secondary or vocational place, and whether you are giving as an individual, a family or a group.

  2. We match

    We match you to a child on the register whose place is unfunded, in the order they were assessed — not by whose photograph appeals most.

  3. You set up giving

    We confirm the exact monthly figure and the payment route. Nothing is charged before you have confirmed it in writing.

  4. You hear back

    A termly report on attendance, results and wellbeing from the case worker, and an annual summary of how the education fund as a whole performed.

Start sponsoring

Register your interest

No payment is taken on this page and no card details are handled anywhere on this site. We reply within five working days with the exact figure and the payment routes available to you.

Giving from Uganda? Say so in the message and we will send mobile money details. Giving from abroad? We will send bank transfer details and confirm receipt in writing.

Questions

What sponsors usually ask

No. Children are matched in the order they were assessed against documented vulnerability criteria. Choosing by photograph would make a child’s appearance a factor in whether they go to school, which we will not do.

Correspondence passes through the case worker and is read first, as safeguarding practice requires. Visits are possible only as part of a planned field week, with vetting completed beforehand, and never one-to-one.

Tell us and we will re-fund the place from the pooled education fund. Sponsorship is a commitment we would like you to keep, but no child’s schooling is ever left to depend on a single individual continuing to pay.

It happens, and the report will say so. The case worker will have recorded why, what was tried, and whether a return is realistic. Where it is not, your sponsorship moves to another child and you are told.

Not ready to commit monthly?

A one-off gift to the education fund buys scholastic materials for a whole class, and is coded and reported in exactly the same way.
