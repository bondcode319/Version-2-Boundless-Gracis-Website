We Heal

Community health outreaches, malnutrition screening, immunisation and maternal referral, safe water points and hygiene promotion — delivered with district health teams, not around them.

We Educate

Sponsorship, fees, scholastic materials and uniforms; early childhood and remedial learning; vocational training and mentorship — with priority to girls and children with disabilities.

We Empower

Village savings and loan associations, enterprise and financial literacy training, start-up grants, climate-smart agriculture and a women’s skilling centre for caregivers.

Who we are

A foundation built around the child, not the programme

Boundless Gracia Children’s Foundation is a non-profit, non-partisan child-focused organisation serving orphaned, abandoned and vulnerable children, children with disabilities, and the caregivers who hold their families together — most often single mothers, grandmothers and elderly guardians. We work where poverty, family breakdown and thin services meet in a child’s life, and we respond with an integrated package rather than one isolated intervention.

Why sponsor a child

Sponsorship is a commitment, not a transaction

A school year is predictable. A child’s health is not. Sponsorship covers fees, uniform and materials on a known calendar — and it also absorbs the unbudgeted week when malaria puts a child in bed, or a guardian falls ill and the household income stops.

That is why we ask for a monthly commitment rather than a one-off gift. Continuity is what keeps a child enrolled through the transition to secondary school, which is exactly where most drop out — and where girls drop out first.

You will receive termly reporting on attendance, results and wellbeing, written by the child’s case worker. We do not publish an identifiable image or personal detail of any child without documented guardian consent.
